How to read this page

  • 27 Gear Sets (up to 4-piece bonuses + unique Chest & Backpack talents) and 37 Brand Sets (up to 3-piece bonuses + named items with Perfect talents) — all 64 sets.
  • Focus badges colour-match the game's core attributes: red = DPS, yellow = Skill/Healer, blue = Tank, purple = Hybrid.
  • Best rolls = secondary attributes to chase on the remaining pieces to stay coherent with the set's own bonuses.
  • Gear set Chest & Backpack talents are shown in purple. Brand set Named items with Perfect talents are shown in green.
