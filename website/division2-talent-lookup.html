Fast sorting rules

  • Only chest & backpack roll talents. Mask / gloves / holster / kneepads — judge on core + secondaries + brand only.
  • For DPS, want a red core (Weapon Damage) plus Crit Chance + Crit Damage secondaries.
  • Crit Chance soft target ~50%, hard cap 60% — anything past 60% CHC is wasted.
  • Before scrapping a near-miss, extract the one great talent/attribute into your library first.
  • Deconstruct, never sell — you get crafting materials back.
  • Leveling stage: keep coherent crit pieces, don't agonise over god rolls yet.

Read me: this covers all 39 rollable weapon talents from The Division Wiki. Three wiki entries are left out because they're locked to specific named/exotic weapons, not random drops: Allegro (Invisible Hand), Extra (Rock'n'Roll) and Jazz Hands. Named weapons also carry "Perfect" versions of these talents — keep those regardless. Verdicts are tuned for a solo level-40 PvE player gearing up, so a "Skip" can still be fine for a niche status or skill build. Exact percentages get nudged by balance patches; confirm god-roll specifics against a live source before you recalibrate. Names match loosely, so partial typing works.
