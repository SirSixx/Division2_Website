Fast triage rules

  • Check Dark Zone exclusive status before deconstructing — Yaahl brand and System Corruption gear set can't be farmed in PvE, so hold onto them.
  • Mask / gloves / holster / kneepads only carry core + 1 secondary — easy to re-farm, deconstruct freely unless the roll is genuinely rare.
  • Chest / backpack carry 2 secondaries + a talent — three variables to hit at once, so these are worth holding onto more carefully, especially off-core-color rolls.
  • Gear-set pieces (green) roll one fewer attribute than high-end (yellow) — easier to replace, so clear these first when you need stash space.
  • Named weapons mostly have a fixed talent (exceptions: TDI "Kard" Custom, The Mop, The White Death) — keep your best roll of each, deconstruct extras.
  • Mods: keep ~3 of each maxed gear mod, 1 of each skill mod (level 40+ only — usable at 30 too). Stockpile extras on an alt, not your stash.
  • A maxed roll isn't automatically worth keeping — judge it against what the build actually needs first.
  • Deconstruct, never sell — you get crafting materials back either way.
DPS
Core: Weapon Damage (Red)
  • 1Critical Hit Chance — to the 60% hard cap
  • 2Critical Hit Damage — everything past the CHC cap goes here
  • 3Headshot Damage — only if the weapon/playstyle is headshot-reliant
  • 4Weapon Handling — filler / QoL, not a damage stat
Brand sets roll 2 secondaries per item vs. 1 on gear sets — easier to stack Crit Chance + Crit Damage on the same piece.
Skill
Core: Skill Tier (Yellow)
  • 1Skill Damage or Skill Haste — pick per build: big single hits want Damage, spam/uptime wants Haste
  • 2Skill Repair — only if the build leans on healing/repair-type skills, self or allies
Skill Damage also scales status-effect DPS (bleed/burn/shock ticks), not just direct skill hits.
Tank
Core: Armor (Blue)
  • 1Hazard Protection — softens status/DoT damage
  • 2Explosive Resistance — softens burst/explosive damage
  • 3Armor Regeneration — sustain between fights
  • 4Health — flat buffer once armor breaks
Crit/weapon stats are wasted here — the tank isn't the one pulling the trigger for damage. Mix depends on what's actually hitting the build in that content.
Support
Core: mixed — depends on skill-support vs. survivability-support lean
  • 1Skill Repair — the core support stat, self and allies both
  • 2Skill Haste — keeps repair/shield skills up more often
  • 3Armor Regeneration / Health — backstop so the support survives long enough to matter
Attribute Glossary

Weapon

Weapon Handling
Accuracy, stability, and reload speed of equipped weapons.
Critical Hit Chance
% chance a shot crits. Hard cap 60% total.
Critical Hit Damage
Bonus damage multiplier applied on a critical hit.
Headshot Damage
Bonus damage applied specifically to headshots.

Skill

Skill Damage
Increases damage from skills, including status-effect DoT ticks.
Skill Repair
Increases healing/repair output from skills, to self and allies.
Skill Haste
Reduces the cooldown/refresh time before a skill can be used again.

Defensive

Armor Regeneration
Passive armor repair per second while in combat, after taking damage.
Hazard Protection
Reduces damage from status effects (bleed, burn, etc).
Health
Increases the health pool used once armor is depleted.
Explosive Resistance
Reduces damage taken from explosives.
